## Notes on source quality and how citation tiers were used

- **Tier 1 (strongest):** IRDS/ITRS roadmap documents `[IRDS2020-ES]`, `[IRDS2021-ES]`, `[IRDS2022-ES]`, `[ITRS2001-PIDS]`; vendor IEDM/ISSCC papers `[Intel22nm-IEDM2012]`, `[IBM-10nmFinFET]`, `[IBM-7nmFinFET-EUV]`; peer-reviewed PDK paper `[ASAP7-2016]`. These are used for load-bearing numeric claims (pitch, CD, node scaling).
- **Tier 2 (structural/topological facts):** Patents `[E]` group — used **only** to confirm *geometric relationships* (e.g., "word line pitch = 3F, bit line pitch = 2F in a 6F2 cell", "active area is diagonally tilted relative to word/bit lines") that are consistent across many independent filings and also appear in Tier-1/Tier-3 sources. No patent is used as the *sole* source for a quantitative value carried into `05_generator_parameters.md` without at least one Tier-1/Tier-3 corroboration.
- **Tier 3 (secondary press):** Used only to corroborate a Tier-1 number or to supply context (history, market framing) — never cited alone for a number that feeds the generator parameter tables.
- Every quantitative parameter in `05_generator_parameters.md` carries a "Supporting references" field listing ≥2 keys from this list, drawn from at least two different tiers/organizations where literature permits. Where independent corroboration was not found, the parameter is explicitly flagged as **single-sourced** or **inferred** rather than presented as fact — see `08_open_questions.md`.
