## E. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Physics Foundation
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Standard SEM reference. Chapters 3–6.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Comprehensive SEM/X-ray microanalysis.
- [J7] Seiler (1983) — Authoritative SE emission review.

### CD-SEM Metrology
- [B7] Postek & Vladar (2007) — CD-SEM in semiconductor industry.
- [J1] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — NIST CD-SEM edge profiles.
- [J6] Bunday et al. (2003) — CD-SEM methodology.

### Monte Carlo
- [B4] Joy (1995) — MC modeling, low-energy BSE yield.
- [J8] Shimizu & Ding (1992) — MC review.
- [J9] Drouin et al. (CASINO) — MC simulation code.

### Parameters
- [T1] NIST SRD 71 — Inelastic mean free path database.
- [J2] Archie (2005) — CD metrology parameters.
