## F. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Process Technology Foundation
- [F1] Plummer (2000) — The standard process textbook. Covers all unit steps.
- [F2] Wolf & Tauber (2002) — Comprehensive process encyclopedia.
- [F4] Quirk & Serda (2001) — Manufacturing-focused process flow.

### Lithography
- [F3] Mack (2007) — Definitive lithography text.
- [F13] Wu & Kumar (2009) — EUV lithography specifics.
- [F16] Levinson (2010) — Practical lithography.

### Etching
- [F14] Gabriel (2010) — Sidewall profile modeling in RIE.
- [F15] Coburn (1979) — Foundational plasma etching paper.

### Deposition and CMP
- [F10] Franssila (2010) — Excellent coverage of deposition and etch.
- [F11] Seshan (2012) — Thin film deposition handbook.
- [F12] Steigerwald (2004) — CMP theory and practice.

### Technology Nodes
- [F8] Taur & Ning (2021) — VLSI device physics; scaling trends.
- [F9] imec (2023) — Advanced node technology.
- IRDS — Official industry roadmap.
