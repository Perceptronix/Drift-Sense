## E. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Physics Foundation
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — The standard reference for SEM physics. Chapters 3–6 cover contrast, yield, resolution.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Comprehensive SEM/X-ray microanalysis. Chapters 3, 4, 7, 10.
- [J7] Seiler (1983) — The definitive review of SE emission.

### Models and Specification
- [B4] Joy (1995) — Monte Carlo modeling; BSE yield model at low energies; PSF.
- [J1] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — CD-SEM edge profile analysis and modeling; the erf+Gaussian model.
- [J2] Archie (2005) — CD metrology fundamentals; instrument parameters.
- [J6] Bunday et al. (2003) — CD-SEM methodology; performance characterization.

### Parameters
- [T1] NIST SRD 71 — Electron inelastic mean free paths (escape depths).
- [B3] Egerton (2016) — Physical constants and scattering cross-sections.
- [B7] Postek & Vladar (2007) — CD-SEM in the semiconductor industry; practical parameters.

### Validation
- [B4] Joy (1995) — Monte Carlo codes (CASINO) for ground truth.
- [J1] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — NIST reference edge profiles.

Throughout Phase 2.5 documents, references are cited in bracket notation (e.g., [B1], [J3], [T1]). Where multiple sources support the same statement, they are listed sequentially [B1][B2][J7].
