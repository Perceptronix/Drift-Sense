## E. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Electron–Solid Interactions and Scattering
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapters 2, 3 on electron scattering and interaction volume.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapters 3, 4 on electron–specimen interactions.
- [B3] Egerton (2016) — Chapter 3 on inelastic scattering.
- [B4] Joy (1995) — Complete Monte Carlo treatment.
- [T2] NIST SRD 64 — Elastic cross-section database.

### Secondary Electrons
- [J1] Seiler (1983) — Comprehensive review of SE emission in SEM.
- [J2] Chung & Everhart (1974) — SE energy distribution model.
- [J4] Lin & Joy (2005) — SE yield data review.
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 3 on SE generation and escape.

### Backscattered Electrons
- [J3] Joy & Luo (1989) — BSE yield empirical model.
- [J5] Arnal et al. (1969) — BSE yield formula.
- [J6] Niedrig (1982) — BSE from thin films.
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 3 on BSE generation.

### Monte Carlo Simulation
- [B4] Joy (1995) — Standard reference for MC in EMS.
- [J7] Hovington et al. (1997) — CASINO code.
- [J8] Drouin et al. (2007) — CASINO V2.42.
- [T1] NIST SRD 71 — IMFP database.

### Semiconductor Materials
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 3 on material dependence.
- [J4] Lin & Joy (2005) — SE yields for various elements.
- [J9] Tanuma et al. (1994) — IMFP calculations for elements and compounds.
