## F. Citation Guidelines for This Repository

1. **Preferred format:** Use the bracket notation [B1], [J2], [W3], [S4] corresponding to the reference codes above.
2. **Multiple sources:** When a statement is supported by multiple references, use sequential order: [B1][B2][J5].
3. **Balanced citations:** Throughout the Phase 2.1 documents, preference has been given to established textbooks (Reimer, Goldstein, Egerton) for fundamental physics, and to manufacturer documentation and NIST publications for semiconductor-specific applications.
4. **Primary vs. secondary:** Where possible, original journal articles [J1]–[J5] are cited for historical and foundational claims. Textbooks [B1]–[B8] are cited for established knowledge. Web resources [W1]–[W12] are cited for current product and operational information.
