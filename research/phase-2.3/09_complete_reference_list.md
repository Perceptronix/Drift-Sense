## E. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Image Formation and Contrast
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapters 4–6 on contrast formation and image interpretation.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapters 4, 7 on image formation and detection.
- [J1] Seiler (1983) — Comprehensive SE contrast review.
- [J3] Villarrubia, Vladar, Postek (2004) — CD-SEM edge profiles and measurement.

### Edge Brightening
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Section 4.2 on edge effects.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Section 7.3 on topographic contrast.
- [J3] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — Quantitative edge profile analysis.
- [J10] Reimer (1993) — Low-voltage edge effects.

### Detector Physics
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 6 on detectors.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 4 on detection systems.
- [W3] Wikipedia — Everhart-Thornley detector principles.

### CD-SEM Profiles and Metrology
- [J3] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — NIST reference for CD-SEM edge profiles.
- [J5] Archie (2005) — CD metrology fundamentals.
- [J6] Bunday et al. (2003) — CD-SEM methodology.
- [J7] Vladar et al. (2007) — CD-SEM advanced applications.
- [B7] Postek & Vladar (2007) — Comprehensive CD-SEM reference.

### Contrast Models
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 4 on contrast theory.
- [B4] Joy (1995) — Monte Carlo approach to contrast.
- [B6] Wells (1974) — Early theoretical treatment.
- [J4] Shimizu & Ding (1992) — MC modeling review.

Throughout Phase 2.3 documents, references are cited using the bracket notation above (e.g., [B1], [J3], [W4]). Multiple sources supporting the same statement are cited sequentially: [B1][J1][J3].
