## E. Key Reference Summary by Topic

### Resolution and Blur
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapters 5, 6 on resolution limits and probe formation.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 3 on beam–specimen interaction and resolution.
- [B3] Egerton (2016) — Chapters 2, 4 on lens aberrations and resolution.
- [J1] Villarrubia et al. (2004) — CD-SEM edge profiles and resolution measurement.
- [J4] Joy & Joy (1996) — Low-voltage SEM resolution limits.

### Noise
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 4 on detector noise.
- [B6] Postek & Vladar (2007) — SNR in CD-SEM applications.
- [W2] Wikipedia — Shot noise theory.

### Charging
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 4 on charging effects.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 7 on charging artifacts.
- [J4] Joy & Joy (1996) — Charging at low kV.

### Artifacts
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 7 on artifact sources.
- [B2] Goldstein et al. (2017) — Chapter 10 on artifacts.
- [J5] Ishitani et al. (1994) — Scan distortion characterization.

### Instrument Performance
- [B1] Reimer (1998) — Chapter 5 on probe formation and current.
- [B6] Postek & Vladar (2007) — CD-SEM performance optimization.
- [J2] Archie (2005) — CD metrology trade-offs.
- [J6] Bunday et al. (2003) — CD-SEM methodology.
