# Citation Documents / Supporting References

These support the assumptions used for blur, shot noise, detector noise, charging artifacts, and contrast behavior.

   - Used for electron imaging noise intuition and contrast interpretation foundations.

2. **Goldstein, Joseph et al.** *Scanning Electron Microscopy and X-ray Microanalysis*. Springer.
   - Used to justify secondary/backscattered electron contrast behavior and detector-driven intensity variation.

3. **Joy, D. C.** “A Database on Electron-Solid Interactions.” *Scanning*.
